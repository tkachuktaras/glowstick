# Roadmap

What's planned for Glowstick. Ideas and votes are welcome in [Discussions](https://github.com/tkachuktaras/glowstick/discussions).

## Next

- **Controller profiles** — everything about a controller in one profile: light bar, touchpad, hotkeys, idle timeouts, input mapping. Save, duplicate, import and export them; give each controller or game its own. Basic and advanced views. *(one profile free, several in Pro)*

## Ideas

- **Night mode** — dim or turn off the light bars on a schedule.
- **Keyboard hotkeys** — switch presets and brightness from the keyboard.
- **Edit a built-in preset** — start your own preset from one of the built-in ones. *(Pro)*
- **Colors from music** — the light bar follows the cover of the song that's playing. *(Pro)*
- **Preset gallery** — browse and download presets other people made. *(Pro)*
- **Notifications as light** — Discord / Telegram messages flash the light bar. *(Pro)*
- **More PC lighting** — Razer Chroma and Corsair iCUE next to OpenRGB. *(Pro)*
- **DualSense extras** — haptics and the mute LED as an indicator. *(Pro)*
- **Xbox controllers** — use them with Glowstick's input mapping. *(Pro)*
- **winget and Scoop** packages.
